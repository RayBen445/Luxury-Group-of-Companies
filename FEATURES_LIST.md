# 62 Premium Restaurant Features for La Tavola Royale

## ENGAGEMENT & INTERACTION (8 Features)
1. **Live Chat with Chef** - Real-time chat for dish customization and cooking questions
2. **WhatsApp Integration** - Direct WhatsApp messaging with restaurant for quick inquiries
3. **Interactive Wine Pairing Guide** - Click dishes to see wine recommendations
4. **Cooking Method Selector** - Show nutrition & cooking styles for each dish
5. **Dietary Preference Filter** - Quick filter by vegan/gluten-free/keto/dairy-free
6. **Favorite Dishes** - Heart icon to save and view favorite dishes
7. **Recent Orders** - Show user's previous orders for quick reordering
8. **Recommendation Engine** - "Based on your likes..." personalized suggestions

## BOOKING & RESERVATIONS (10 Features)
9. **Calendar Booking** - Visual calendar picker instead of dropdown
10. **Live Table Availability** - Real-time table status showing available tables
11. **Table Layout Selector** - Choose specific table location (window, corner, etc)
12. **Party Composition** - Specify guest types (adults/kids/elderly/VIP)
13. **Special Occasion Marker** - Flag birthdays, anniversaries, proposals for special treatment
14. **Pre-Order Meals** - Order meals before arrival for faster service
15. **Arrival Confirmation** - SMS/Email confirmation when guest is 30min away
16. **Wait List** - Join waiting list if fully booked
17. **Booking History** - View past and future reservations with confirmation numbers
18. **Booking Modifications** - Edit or cancel with clear policies and refund info

## ORDERING & PAYMENT (10 Features)
19. **Delivery Tracking Map** - Live GPS tracking of delivery with ETA
20. **Multiple Payment Methods** - Apple Pay, Google Pay, Stripe, Venmo, etc
21. **Loyalty Program** - Points for orders/reservations with rewards tiers
22. **Referral Bonuses** - Invite friends for discounts and rewards
23. **Promotional Code Input** - Enter promo codes at checkout
24. **Bundle Deals** - Meal packages at discounts (dinner for two, etc)
25. **Payment Plans** - Finance larger catering orders
26. **Invoice Download** - PDF receipts and invoices for business expenses
27. **Tip Calculator** - Suggested tipping amounts with custom options
28. **Price Comparison** - Show before/after discount prices clearly

## CONTENT & STORYTELLING (9 Features)
29. **Chef's Biography** - Detailed chef profiles with credentials and certifications
30. **Dish Origin Story** - Historical context for signature dishes
31. **Ingredient Sourcing** - Where ingredients come from with supplier links
32. **Video Tutorials** - How to cook certain dishes at home from our chefs
33. **Behind-the-Scenes Videos** - Kitchen prep, plating, and food creation process
34. **Event Gallery** - Photos from recent restaurant events and functions
35. **Blog/Articles** - Food culture, recipes, dining tips, seasonal updates
36. **Testimonial Videos** - Guest reviews with video testimonials
37. **Awards & Accolades** - Display certifications, awards, and media mentions

## INTERACTIVE FEATURES (8 Features)
38. **Photo Upload Gallery** - Guests post their meal photos with social sharing
39. **Rating & Review System** - 5-star reviews with photo and video uploads
40. **Menu Voting** - Vote on new dishes to add to permanent menu
41. **Quiz/Personality Test** - "What's Your Ideal Dish?" personality quiz
42. **Interactive Nutrition Calculator** - Calories/macros/allergens per dish
43. **Allergen Checker** - Alert system for common allergens
44. **Customization Builder** - Build your own dish with options
45. **Comparison Tool** - Compare similar dishes side-by-side

## CUSTOMER SERVICE (9 Features)
46. **FAQ Chatbot** - AI-powered Q&A for common questions
47. **Virtual Tour** - 360° restaurant walkthrough and ambiance preview
48. **Accessibility Features** - Text sizing, contrast modes, screen reader support
49. **Contact Form with Priorities** - Different forms for different needs (complaints, inquiries, etc)
50. **Email Newsletter** - Weekly specials and updates
51. **SMS Alerts** - Order updates via text messages
52. **Birthday Special** - Auto-reminder with birthday discount
53. **Loyalty Tier Status** - Show VIP, Premium, Standard membership levels
54. **Gift Card Store** - Buy, redeem, and manage digital gift cards

## OPERATIONAL (9 Features)
55. **Availability Calendar** - Show fully booked dates and open slots
56. **Staff Directory** - See who's working today (sommelier, chefs, etc)
57. **Special Menu** - Holiday or seasonal specials with countdown timer
58. **Catering Services** - Group event booking form and menu selection
59. **Private Event Inquiry** - Inquiry form for private dining experiences
60. **Maintenance Alerts** - Show if restaurant is closed or has limited hours
61. **Rate Limiting** - Prevent abuse on order system
